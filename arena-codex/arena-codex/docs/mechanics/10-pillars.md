# The Coliseum of Heroes: 10 Life Pillars

> **"True power isn't measured by bench press alone. A balanced hero thrives across all domains of life."**

## Overview
The **Coliseum of Heroes** is a weekly self-assessment tool evaluating balance across 10 vital life domains:

1. **Strength & Physical Power**: Functional movement, resistance training, and physical capability.
2. **Mental Health**: Emotional regulation, self-compassion, and stress management.
3. **Nutrition & Fuel**: Sustainable, plant-forward nourishment without guilt.
4. **Mindset & Resilience**: Cognitive adaptability and growth mindset.
5. **Recovery & Sleep**: Sleep quality and nervous system downregulation.
6. **Relationships & Community**: Healthy interpersonal connections and belonging.
7. **Financial Wellness**: Financial stability and resource management.
8. **Environment & Space**: Supportive living and working surroundings.
9. **Purpose & Calling**: Alignment with core values and meaningful pursuits.
10. **Joy & Play**: Unstructured fun, hobbies, and genuine playfulness.
