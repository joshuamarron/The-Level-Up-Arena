# Quest Taxonomy & Service Architecture

> **"Every hero needs a quest log tailored to their current level and objective."**

## 1. Main Quests (Ultimate Edition Coaching)
- Flagship 1-on-1 whole-human coaching (3-Month, 6-Month, 12-Month options).
- Integrates custom strength programming, plant-forward nutrition, mindset calls, and 10-pillar tracking.

## 2. Side Quests (Targeted Modules)
- **Fitness & Movement Quest**
- **Endurance & OCR Quest** (Spartan Race & DEKA preparation)
- **Plant-Based Nutrition Quest**
- **Mind & Body Balance Quest**

## 3. Experience Points (XP) & Skill Trees
- Non-competitive personal progress tracking measuring self-improvement over time.
