# Coach J.V. Marron — Founder & Head Coach

> **"I rebuilt my own body and life from the ground up. Now, I help others discover their own strength."**

## Origin Story: The Comeback Quest
Following a severe motorcycle accident that left him with a metal rod in his leg, Coach J.V. Marron reframed his physical rehabilitation as an epic quest. He restored his physical capacity, competed in Spartan DEKA events, and built **The Level Up! Arena**.
