# Client Autonomy & The Coaching Hierarchy

> **"We may be the experts in our fields, but our clients are the experts on themselves."**

## The Three-Role Hierarchy
1. **Partner First**: Coach and client collaborate as co-op partners. The client retains full authority over their decisions and pace.
2. **Mentor Second**: Providing perspective, encouragement, accountability, and education when motivation drops or plateaus occur.
3. **Expert Always**: Delivering evidence-based recommendations and professional strategies while respecting client autonomy.

## The Why Method & Client Independence
- **The Five Whys**: Conversational probing to uncover intrinsic motivation.
- **Fostering Independence**: Teaching warriors how to self-direct their health and habits for lifelong autonomy.
